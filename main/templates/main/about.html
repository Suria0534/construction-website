About Us

A Momin Engineering & Construction is a trusted name in the construction industry, proudly serving Bangladesh since 2012. With over a decade of experience, we specialize in delivering high-quality construction solutions across commercial, industrial, and public sectors.

Our expertise ranges from steel construction and building retrofitting to commercial complexes, industrial facilities, and government projects. We are also a reliable supplier of a wide range of construction materials, ensuring that every project we undertake is completed with top-grade resources and craftsmanship.

At A Momin Engineering & Construction, we believe in building more than just structures — we build trust, safety, and long-lasting relationships. Our team of engineers, architects, and skilled professionals work with a commitment to innovation, quality, and timely delivery.

Whether you're planning a new development or upgrading an existing structure, we are here to bring your vision to life — one strong foundation at a time.
